# Total Bill Calculator with Service Fee and Sales Tax

This program calculates the total bill amount after including a service fee and sales tax.

**How it works:**
1. The user enters the original bill value.
2. The program applies a service fee (1.1×) and a sales tax (1.16×).
3. The final total bill is displayed: `total_bill = Bill_value * service_fee * sales_tax`.

This program demonstrates the use of constants, arithmetic operations, and basic input/output in C++.
